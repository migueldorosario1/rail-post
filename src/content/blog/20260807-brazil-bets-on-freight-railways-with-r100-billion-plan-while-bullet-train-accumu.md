The federal government projects eight railway auctions in 2026 and signs an agreement with China to connect the Atlantic to the Pacific, but the SP-RJ high-speed train still lacks environmental licensing and has postponed works.

<p>Brazilian railway modernity does not lie in speed; it lies in the capacity to transport production along rails that finally reach the sea. While the bullet train between São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro accumulates eighteen years of promises and no tracks built, freight corridors advance with tens of billions of reais in investments and state planning.</p>
<p>The National Railway Plan, presented to President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva by Transport Minister Renan Filho, foresees around R$100 billion in investments and the expansion of 4,700 kilometers of railway network. According to <a href='https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/economia/macroeconomia/com-investimento-de-r-100-bi-governo-lancara-plano-nacional-de-ferrovias/' target='_blank'>CNN Brasil</a>, the initiative includes five major projects that only require final adjustments to be launched, with the Union funding 20% to 30% of the contributions to ensure the viability of each undertaking.</p>
<p>The East-West Corridor, backbone of the plan, will link the West-East Integration Railway (FIOL) to the Midwest Integration Railway (FICO) in a route of approximately 2,400 kilometers between Ilhéus (BA) and Lucas do Rio Verde (MT). FIOL 2 will have 71% of its works built by the public sector, while the winning company of the auction will take on the remainder of the construction and the entirety of FIOL 3.</p>
<p>Still on the Pacific route, Brazil and China signed a memorandum of understanding on July 7, 2025 for studies of a transcontinental railway connecting the port of Ilhéus to the port of Chancay in Peru. According to <a href='https://g1.globo.com/economia/noticia/2025/07/08/ferrovia-para-ligar-o-atlantico-ao-pacifico-veja-o-que-se-sabe-ate-agora-do-projeto-do-brasil-com-a-china.ghtml' target='_blank'>G1</a>, the new route could reduce cargo transport time from Brazil to Asia from 40 to 28 days, with significant impacts on agribusiness competitiveness.</p>
<p>The National Secretary of Railway Transport, Leonardo Ribeiro, classified the initiative as a strategic step and a technical and diplomatic move to bring continents closer. He recalled that similar studies conducted between 2015 and 2016 did not advance due to the political scenario, but now there is more technical maturity and available infrastructure.</p>
<p>At the other end of the country, the extension of the North-South Railway for another 477 kilometers, from Açailândia (MA) to the port of Vila do Conde (PA), will create a logistical alternative for grains that currently compete for space with iron ore on the Carajás Railway. The Southeast Railway Ring, about 300 kilometers between Vitória (ES) and Itaboraí (RJ), will connect the network of the Vitória-Minas Railway to the MRS Logística network, increasing the fluidity of the country's main export axis.</p>
<p>The Transnordestina, with another 600 kilometers of tracks to the connection with the North-South in Estreito (MA), is expected to be delivered in 2026 or 2027. The Ferrogrão, a 933-kilometer corridor between Sinop (MT) and Itaituba (PA), remains dependent on conciliation in the Supreme Federal Court due to impacts on conservation units such as the Jamanxim National Park.</p>
<p>The contrast with high-speed passenger mode is glaring. According to <a href='https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/brasil/promessa-do-pt-obras-adiadas-trem-bala-sao-paulo-rio-de-janeiro/' target='_blank'>Gazeta do Povo</a>, the bullet train between São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro had its works postponed to 2028 and accumulates eighteen years of promises without a single track built, with a current estimated cost of R$60 billion and without environmental license.</p>
<p>The economist and president of Inter.B Consultoria Internacional de Negócios, Cláudio Frischtak, criticizes the project and advocates for a medium-speed train with shared track use for freight at night. He recalls that Brazilian railway engineering operates at speeds of 80 to 100 km/h, far from the over 300 km/h required by the TAV, and that only four countries master the technology on a large scale.</p>
<p>The director-secretary of the Brazilian Association of Railway Preservation (ABPF), Thomaz Schoeffel, warns that crossing the Serra do Mar and urban interferences make the project unviable without massive importation of specialized engineering. He also states that the country does not have railway engineering prepared for this technological leap, which would further increase implementation and maintenance costs.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, the federal government projects eight railway auctions in 2026, according to a <a href='https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/infra/com-foco-em-ferrovias-governo-avanca-nos-primeiros-projetos/' target='_blank'>CNN Brasil</a> report, although the first, the Minas Rio Corridor, already has its public notice delayed since January. The director-general of the National Land Transport Agency (ANTT), Guilherme Sampaio, explained that the public call will be the preferred instrument for shortlines, short returned networks that can house freight, passenger, or mixed corridors.</p>
<p>The division of the South Network into three lots (Paraná-SC, Rio Grande and Mercosur) is part of the rebidding strategy to increase the attractiveness of projects. The executive secretary of the Ministry of Transport, George Santoro, admitted that the public notice for the Minas Rio Corridor, scheduled for January, should be postponed to May and the auction to August.</p>
<p>In practice, the bet on freight railways already moves compositions of unit trains formed by hopper cars pulled by diesel-electric locomotives, promising to transform the outflow of harvests through the country's interior. Intermodality gains strength with the connection between rails, waterways, and port terminals, shortening continental distances without depending on high speed.</p>
<p>National railway modernity, therefore, does not pursue the glamour of the bullet train, but the silent robustness of a granted network that finally begins to fulfill its strategic role. While the São Paulo-Rio project remains on paper, freight corridors advance with tracks, contracts, and the promise of continental integration between the Atlantic and the Pacific.</p>
