National Railway Plan orchestrates largest railway cycle in decades with Atlantic-Pacific route and 4.7 thousand km of tracks, but rolling stock remains undefined.

<p>The tracks are the material geometry of the future. Brazil begins to redesign its logistic map from a premise that combines heavy engineering, territorial sovereignty, and economic calculation.</p>
<p>After decades of neglect, freight rail returns to the center of state policy with a plan that mobilizes R$ 100 billion. There are 4.7 thousand kilometers of new tracks planned in the National Railway Plan, presented by Transport Minister Renan Filho to President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.</p>
<p>The most ambitious project is the East-West Corridor, a 2,400-kilometer link between the West-East Integration Railway (Fiol) and the Central-West Integration Railway (Fico). It will connect the port of Ilhéus, in Bahia, to Lucas do Rio Verde, in Mato Grosso, a strategic outlet for grain and mineral shipments.</p>
<p>But the major geopolitical news came with the memorandum signed between Brazil and China in July 2025, as reported by <a href='https://g1.globo.com/economia/noticia/2025/07/08/ferrovia-para-ligar-o-atlantico-ao-pacifico-veja-o-que-se-sabe-ate-agora-do-projeto-do-brasil-com-a-china.ghtml' target='_blank'>G1</a>. The agreement provides for studies to extend these tracks to the port of Chancay, in Peru, crossing the South American continent from the Atlantic to the Pacific.</p>
<p>The transcontinental route would reduce transportation time for cargo from Brazil to Asia from 40 to 28 days, according to estimates by the Peruvian government. The engineering of the route is not yet defined, but Infra S.A. and the China Railway Economic and Planning Research Institute will have five years to assess technical, economic, and environmental feasibility.</p>
<p>National Secretary of Railway Transport Leonardo Ribeiro described the initiative as a technical and diplomatic move to bring continents closer. The combination of rail and sea modes would replace the current road model that makes national production more expensive.</p>
<p>The National Railway Plan, detailed by <a href='https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/economia/macroeconomia/com-investimento-de-r-100-bi-governo-lancara-plano-nacional-de-ferrovias/' target='_blank'>CNN Brasil</a>, includes five structuring projects in addition to the East-West Corridor. The extension of the North-South Railway, with 477 kilometers, will connect Açailândia to the port of Vila do Conde, in Pará, easing dependence on the Carajás Railway.</p>
<p>The Southeastern Railway Ring, about 300 kilometers long, will connect Vitória to Itaboraí, integrating the networks of Vale and MRS Logística. The Transnordestina, a project dragging on since 2010, will finally have a connection with the North-South line in Estreito, Maranhão, adding another 600 kilometers to the system.</p>
<p>The Ferrogrão, with 933 kilometers between Sinop and Itaituba, is the most sensitive project. It depends on conciliation in the Supreme Federal Court due to impacts on conservation units, such as the Jamanxim National Park.</p>
<p>While the routes advance on paper, the 2026 agenda foresees eight railway auctions, but the first is already delayed. The bidding notice for the Minas Rio Corridor, expected for January, has not yet been published, and the auction may be postponed to August, according to Executive Secretary George Santoro.</p>
<p>The director-general of the National Land Transport Agency (ANTT), Guilherme Sampaio, explained to <a href='https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/infra/com-foco-em-ferrovias-governo-avanca-nos-primeiros-projetos/' target='_blank'>CNN Brasil</a> that public calls will be the main instrument for smaller networks, the so-called shortlines. The idea is to replicate the port model, with a bidding notice, reference project, and analysis by the Federal Court of Accounts.</p>
